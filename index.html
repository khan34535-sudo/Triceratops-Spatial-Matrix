Readout

Linked to the age slider. Older animals show more growth lines, more internal remodelling and, at maturity, a tightly packed outer band.

Legend

  1. Medullary cavity — marrow space with trabeculae
  2. Secondary osteon — remodelled Haversian system with cement line
  3. Primary osteon / vascular canal — in fast-growing woven-parallel (fibrolamellar-type) bone
  4. LAG — line of arrested growth, roughly one per growth pause
  5. Osteocyte lacunae — tiny cell cavities
  6. EFS — external fundamental system: growth has slowed or stopped

What it tells us

Age at death, growth rate, when maturity was reached, and how the animal's body responded to stress or injury.

Caveat

Remodelling erases the earliest growth lines, so counts underestimate true age; researchers correct for this by comparing bones across a growth series.

Pathology under the lens

Infection or healed trauma shows up as disorganised bone, extra vascular canals and reactive new bone.

Layer 08 · Facts

Key facts, diet & skeletal analysis

~8–9 mbody length
~6–12 testimated mass
~2–2.5 mlargest skull lengths
68–66 MaMaastrichtian
  • Name means "three-horned face" (Marsh, 1889); T. horridus and T. prorsus are widely recognised.
  • It shared its world with Tyrannosaurus; healed bite marks show some prey survived.
  • Skin impressions show large polygonal scales with scattered bigger bumps.
  • Whether Torosaurus is an old Triceratops is debated (Scannella & Horner 2010 vs Longrich & Field 2012).
  • Diet: low-growing ferns, cycads, palms and flowering shrubs, cropped by the beak and sliced by the tooth batteries.

Strictly, bioarchaeology studies human remains; for dinosaurs the same toolkit sits under palaeo-osteology and palaeopathology — bone histology, CT and photogrammetry, tooth microwear, isotopes, taphonomy and pathology surveys.

Notes

Disclaimers & sources

What is illustrative

  • The chemistry bars are illustrative, not measured values.
  • Geography is approximate: the land outline and the Western Interior Seaway are stylised.
  • Anatomy is a simplified teaching model — not scan data or photographs. Vascular, organ and thermal layers are inferred from living relatives (birds and crocodilians).
  • The bone-histology image is a procedurally generated schematic, not a real thin section.
  • The population figures come from an adjustable model, not a measured count.
  • Citations and institution details should be verified before outreach.
